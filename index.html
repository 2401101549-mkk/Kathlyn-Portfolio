About Me

Hello! I'm Kathlyn Khae B. Miana, a first-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) student at Bukidnon State University.

I'm someone who enjoys exploring new ideas, learning different skills, and discovering how technology and creativity can come together.

My interests include programming, web development, cybersecurity, and digital design. Beyond technology, I enjoy drawing, crochet, music, and experimenting with creative projects, including AI-assisted music.
